Each page replays one real run. Every number comes from the run's report and ledger.

案例总页：谁在说谎、最短书单、合租分配 / Cases: who is lying, shortest reading list, flat-share matching

三个程序各在真机上跑了一次，页面逐步展示每一次判断。三个案子都没有完全定案：谁在说谎的三个案子里都仍有拿不准的证词对，A、B 两案圈出的两人里含真凶，C 案程序认定周琳，而出题设定的是韩梅；最短书单选出 6 篇，仍有 5 个概念没有一篇讲清；合租分配在同分时换 8 种排序，得到 4 种稳定匹配。

Three programs, each run once on the real backend, with every judgment shown step by step. None of the three cases is fully settled. In all three who-is-lying cases some testimony pairs stay uncertain; cases A and B circle two people that include the culprit, while in case C the program names Zhou Lin and the case design says Han Mei. The shortest reading list picks 6 articles and 5 concepts remain unexplained by any of them. In the flat-share case, 8 tie-break orderings give 4 different stable matchings.

杭州聚餐 / Hangzhou dinner

五个人在杭州约一顿饭，程序用 OpenStreetMap 的真实餐厅数据筛店、排座位、分组。每组约 290 次判断、花费不到 0.01 美元。语义判断对忌口几乎都拿不准，选出的店多半缺菜系标签，结果照实保留。这一页仍在修改，之后会更新。

Five people plan a dinner in Hangzhou; the program filters restaurants from real OpenStreetMap data, seats guests and splits groups. Each group takes about 290 judgments for under $0.01. Nearly all dietary-clash judgments came back uncertain, and most chosen venues lack a cuisine tag; the result is kept as it came out. This page is still being revised and will be updated.

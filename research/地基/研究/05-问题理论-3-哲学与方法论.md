## 1. Collingwood，《An Essay on Metaphysics》(1940, Oxford: Clarendon Press)

**①出处**：R.G. Collingwood, *An Essay on Metaphysics*, Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1940. 实际打开：
https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.187414 （直接下载 `_djvu.txt`）

**②核心对象与结论**：把"命题"(proposition) 和"预设"(presupposition) 定义为同一句话在问答逻辑中扮演的
两种不同角色，而非由内容决定。核心命题：
> Prop. 1. "Every statement that anybody ever makes is made in answer to a question."
> Prop. 2. "Every question involves a presupposition."
> Def. 1. "Let that which is stated (i.e. that which can be true or false) be called a proposition, and let
> stating it be called propounding it."

绝对预设 (absolute presupposition) 与相对预设 (relative presupposition) 的区分是全书核心：相对预设可以
被更高一级的问题验证（本身能变成某个问题的答案）；绝对预设永远只充当预设、不可能变成任何问题的答案，
因而不可验证，也不适用真假：
> Def. 6. "An absolute presupposition is one which stands, relatively to all questions to which it is
> related, as a presupposition, never as an answer."
> Prop. 5. "Absolute presuppositions are not propositions."

**③好问题标准 / 前提不成立时怎么办**：一个问题"成立"(arise) 当且仅当其直接预设"被做出"（不要求为真，
见 Prop. 3："The logical efficacy of a supposition does not depend upon the truth of what is supposed... but
only on its being supposed."）。预设不被做出时，问题"does not arise"：
> Def. 2. "To say that a question 'does not arise' is the ordinary English way of saying that it involves a
> presupposition which is not in fact being made."
> "A question that 'does not arise' is thus a nonsense question: not intrinsically nonsensical, but
> nonsensical in relation to its context, and specifically to its presuppositions."

经典例证（"你是否已经不再打老婆"）：若对方没有打老婆的预设行为习惯（预设未被做出），"has he stopped"这个
问题"does not arise"，不是问出了假答案，而是问错了问题本身。对绝对预设，"是否为真""有何证据""如何证明"
这类追问统统是"无意义问题"(nonsense question)（p.33）。

**④生成下一问题的规则**：
> Def. 3. "The fact that something causes a certain question to arise I call the 'logical efficacy' of that
> thing."

某个命题（哪怕只是被假设，不必被断言为真）"使"另一个问题得以产生。全书没有给出机械算法式的"如何从一个
问题算出下一个问题"，但给出了结构性约束：一个问题的直接预设只有一个（"Directly or immediately, any
given question involves one presupposition and only one"），这个预设自身又有更高一级的（间接）预设，
由此形成一条可回溯的预设链，直到触及不可再问的绝对预设为止。

**⑤待映射**："预设成立"不要求预设为真、只要求预设"被做出"，给 J++ 的前提诊断提供了明确检验对象；绝对
预设"触底即停"给"何时不该再往下问"提供了一个哲学原型——停下不是因为问题被回答了，而是因为再问是范畴错误。

**⑥未核实**：*An Autobiography* (1939) 第五章"Question and Answer"原文未获取（archive.org 该书借阅受限，
`access-restricted-item: true`，程序化 search-inside 接口返回 "item not available"）。以下内容仅转引自
SEP "Robin George Collingwood"词条（级别③，非一手，不作为引文使用）：Collingwood 提出"哲学分析"的任务
是从命题回溯到其所答问题、再回溯到问题得以产生的（绝对）预设。二手文献中常提到的考古学"探沟"类比，
本次未在任何实际打开的来源中见到，故不提及、不采信。

## 2. Bromberger，《On What We Know We Don't Know》(1992, U Chicago Press) 及 "Why-Questions" (1966)

**①出处**：Sylvain Bromberger, *On What We Know We Don't Know: Explanation, Theory, Linguistics, and How
Questions Shape Them*, Chicago: University of Chicago Press, 1992. 实际打开：
https://web.stanford.edu/group/cslipublications/cslipublications/bromberger-corpus/On-What-We-Know-We-Dont-Know.pdf
（全书237页公开PDF）。另核对 "Why-Questions" (1966), 收录于 R. Colodny (ed.) *Mind and Cosmos*：
http://www.fitelson.org/290/bromberger.pdf

**②核心对象与结论**：把"不知道自己不知道什么"这件事本身对象化为两种不同的认知困境（predicament）。
p-predicament（p 取自 puzzled/perplexed）：
> "A is in a p-predicament with regard to Q if and only if, on A's views, Q admits of a right answer, but A
> can think of no answer to which, on A's views, there are no decisive objections." (ch.1, p.29)

b-predicament（无法凭空想出任何候选答案，与 p-predicament 不同——p-predicament是"想得出候选但都被自己
的背景知识否决"，b-predicament是"根本想不出候选"）：
> "A statement of the form 'A is in a b-predicament with regard to Q'... is true if and only if the question
> mentioned in it admits of a right answer, but that answer is beyond what the person mentioned can conceive,
> can think of, can imagine." (ch.1, p.36-37)

两者可分离：Kepler 拒绝接受椭圆轨道正确答案，是"陷入 p-predicament 而不陷入 b-predicament"的例子（他能
想出正确答案，但被自己的信念否决了）。

**③好问题标准 / 前提不成立时怎么办**：为 why-questions（"Why is it the case that p?"）给出形式化的正确
答案判据，核心是"abnormic law"（反常律，形如"没有 F 除非...的规律"）与"antonymic predicates"（对立谓词）：
> "b is the correct answer to the why-question whose presupposition is a if and only if (1) there is an
> abnormic law L... and a is an instantiation of one of L's antonymic predicates; (2) b is a member of a set
> of premises that together with L constitute a deductive nomological explanation whose conclusion is a;
> (3) the remaining premises together with the general rule completed by L constitute a deduction... whose
> conclusion is a contrary of a; (4) the general rule completed by L has the property that if one of the
> conjuncts in the antecedent is dropped, the new general rule cannot be completed by an abnormic law."
> ("Why-Questions" 1966, 已与 SEP "Questions" §3.1 转述交叉核对一致)

前提不成立（预设为假）时，why-question 无正确答案；在处理范围上，本书明确排除了涉及人类意图/心理状态的
以及本身无正确答案的 why-questions。但本书（第2章）对"候选答案都被否决、卡在 p-predicament"这一情形，
给出了明确的下一步机制——**不是改问，而是把"某个预设的反面"作为一个可考虑的假说提出来**：
> "let Q be such a question, but let there be no known answer not ruled out, and let S be a contrary of
> some presupposition of Q. Let S also be compatible with the facts and with accepted doctrine. Then there
> exists a presumption in behalf of S." (ch.2, p.70-71)

并举了一个具体的科学史例子：当"为什么迈克尔逊-莫雷实验测不到地球相对以太的运动"这个问题所有候选答案都
被排除时，"以太存在"这一预设的反面（即狭义相对论的出发点）就获得了初始的"可考虑性"(presumption)。
他还给出了一条"三选一"的处境刻画：
> "Either you accept them [known-not-ruled-out answers], or you accept answers known to be false, or you
> freeze in a p-predicament." (ch.2, p.70)

**④生成下一问题的规则**：给出了一条明确的、可直接借用的生成规则——"什么使一个命题足以催生一个问题"：
> "[A] proposition P can give rise to a question Q only if it is a presupposition of Q, and it is a
> presupposition of Q only if A's having a correct answer would entail that P is true... Very roughly, P
> must be a conjunction of all the presuppositions or must entail such a conjunction." (ch.2, p.70-72)

即：单个必要条件不足以催生问题（"There are atoms"不足以催生"氧原子的平均质量是多少"），必须是全部预设的
合取才行。

**⑤待映射**：(a) p/b-predicament 的区分可直接映射为 J++"拿不准"出口内部的两种子类型——"候选想得出但都
被材料否决"(p) vs "根本无从想候选"(b)，二者可能需要不同的追问策略；(b) "生成问题的充分条件=全部预设的
合取"给"J++ 如何从一段材料的断言批量生成合法问题"提供了一个可形式化检验的必要条件。

**⑥未核实**：本书第4章"Questions"（关于翻译对等的语言学两条 requirement）与第5-9章（Forms of Ignorance /
Rational Ignorance / linguistics 两章）仅定位未精读；"Why-Questions"原文其余部分（H-关系、"预设是否必须
令人惊讶"的争论）因双栏扫描OCR噪音大，未逐段核实。

## 3. van Fraassen，《The Scientific Image》(1980, Oxford: Clarendon Press) 第5章 "The Pragmatics of Explanation"

**①出处**：Bas C. van Fraassen, *The Scientific Image*, Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1980, ch.5（原书目录
"5. THE PRAGMATICS OF EXPLANATION"，p.97起；SEP "20th Century Theories of Scientific Explanation"§6.2
称其为"Chapter Six"，与本次核实的原书目录不一致，以本次直接核对的原书目录为准）. 实际打开：
https://archive.org/details/the-scientific-image-bas.-c.-fraassen （全书 `_djvu.txt`，未标记借阅限制）

**②核心对象与结论**：把 why-question 定义为一个三元组，解释被重新定义为"回答一个问题"：
> "the why-question Q... will be determined by three factors: The topic P, the contrast-class
> X = {P, P1,...,Pk,...}, The relevance relation R... Q = ⟨P, X, R⟩." (p.142-143)
> "The discussion of explanation went wrong at the very beginning when explanation was conceived of as a
> relationship like description: a relation between theory and fact. Really it is a three-term relation,
> between theory, fact, and context... Since an explanation is an answer, it is evaluated vis-à-vis a
> question, which is a request for information." (p.156)

**③好问题标准 / 前提不成立时怎么办（本任务最关键的一条原文依据）**：van Fraassen 明确把"问题是否成立"
(arise) 与"预设是否为真"这两个此前被等同的概念拉开：
> "In the general theory of questions, the following were equated: question Q arises, all the presuppositions
> of Q are true. The former means that Q is not to be rejected as mistaken, the latter that Q has some true
> answer... [I]n the case of why-questions... this drives a wedge between the two concepts." (p.145)

相对背景知识 K 的正式定义：
> "whether or not the question genuinely arises, depends on whether or not K implies the central
> presupposition. As long as the central presupposition is not part of what is assumed or agreed to in this
> context, the why-question does not arise at all." "the question will still arise, provided K does not imply
> that all those propositions are false... this is very different from 'all the presuppositions are true'."
> (p.145-146)

**④生成下一问题的规则**：本章没有给出"从一个问题生成下一个问题"的规则（它关注的是单个 why-question 的
结构与评估，不是问题链）；给出的是答案评估的三维度：候选本身是否可能为真、是否比对照类里其他成员更有
利于支持 topic、是否被别的候选答案"屏蔽"(screened off)。van Fraassen 自己承认，用来评估答案的背景知识
子集 K(Q) 该如何从整体 K 中选取，"Neither the other authors nor I can say much about it"(p.147)——即他
自己明确承认未解决"该用哪部分材料来评估"这个问题。

**⑤待映射**：("K 蕴含预设为假／K 蕴含预设但答案未定／K 蕴含预设且答案可判定"这三分，可直接对应 J++
"不做/拿不准/做"的出口结构；K(Q) 该如何从整体材料中选取，是 van Fraassen 自己承认未解决的缺口，与
Kitcher & Salmon 对 relevance relation R 不设限的批评（见下条SEP）是同一个缺口的两种表述，待映射。

**⑥未核实**：本书其余各章（第1-4、6章，关于建构经验主义整体论证）未读；van Fraassen 对"如何选取 K(Q)"
在后续论文中是否有补充讨论，未核实。

## 5. Hintikka，互动式提问模型（interrogative model of inquiry）中"前提"与"策略"部分

**①出处**：Jaakko Hintikka, "A Spectrum of Logics of Questioning", *Philosophica* 35(1), 1985, pp.135-150.
实际打开：https://www.philosophica.ugent.be/article/id/82548/download/pdf/ （期刊官网开放获取PDF）。
说明：任务要求覆盖 Hintikka 苏格拉底认识论里"问题的前提"与"策略"部分；专著 *Socratic Epistemology*
(2007) 第4章 "Presuppositions and Other Limitations of Inquiry" 在 Cambridge Core 付费墙后，本次未直接
核实，改核实这篇覆盖同一思想、开放获取的 1985 年论文。

**②核心对象与结论**：把探究形式化为"提问者"(Inquirer) 向"自然"(Nature) 提问的博弈，每次移动要么是演绎
步骤要么是提问步骤。

**③好问题标准 / 前提不成立时怎么办**：提问的形式规则是"先立预设、后发问"：
> "Before the Inquirer may ask a question, its presupposition must occur in the left column of the
> subtableau in question." (p.136)

传统逻辑中的"乞题"(petitio principii) 与"多重问题谬误"(fallacy of many questions，如"你是否已经不再
打老婆") 被重新解释为对这条规则的违反，而非推理错误：
> "The reason why petitio principii violates my game rules is that a question must not be asked by the
> Inquirer before its presupposition has been established." "What is involved in this 'fallacy' is a
> violation of the requirement that the presupposition of a wh-question must be established before the
> question is asked." (p.138-139)

**④生成下一问题的规则**：没有给出机械算法，反而给出一个诚实的负结果——策略选择本身不可机械化：
> "no values ('utilities') can in the last analysis be assigned to individual moves in the game, only to
> (complete) strategies." (p.137-138，即单个提问动作无法独立评估好坏，只有贯穿整条探究的完整策略才能被
> 评价)
> "It is known from proof-theoretical studies that the crucial consideration in deductive strategy selection
> is the selection of an existentially quantified formula to be instantiated... these existential
> instantiations are precisely analogous to the selection of the wh-question to be asked... the optimal
> deductive strategies have been shown not to be mechanical (recursive) in general... empirical discovery is
> not subject to mechanical rules, either, at least not in the unrestricted case." (p.142-144)

**⑤待映射**："先立预设、后发问"可直接对应 J++ 每道题发问前先诊断前提是否成立的机制；Hintikka 自己承认
无限制情形下问题选择无递归算法，是 J++ 用校准概率+启发式去趟一条实践可行路径（而非声称已解决可判定性
问题）的一个诚实边界参照。

**⑥未核实**：Hintikka & Halonen (1995)（why-questions 在互动式提问模型下的发展，SEP §3.4 提到）；
*Socratic Epistemology* (2007) 专著第4章完整论证（付费墙，仅见标题）。definitory rules（界定规则）与
strategic principles（策略规则）这组术语对本文未直接使用"definitory"一词；调研过程中确认团队"第一线"
报告（`05-问题理论-1-问题逻辑本体.md` §6.2）已用 Hintikka 1999 的原文短引核实了这组区分（国际象棋类比，
标页 p.2/p.34），本文不重复抄录，只在④中引用了这篇1985年论文里不同的、"无一般算法"这一负面结果，与
第一线内容互补而非重复。

> Is the condition sufficient to determine the unknown? Or is it insufficient? Or redundant? Or
> contradictory?"

**④生成下一问题的规则（任务要求逐条抄）**——"拟定计划"阶段的追问清单：
> "Find the connection between the data and the unknown. You may be obliged to consider auxiliary problems
> if an immediate connection cannot be found. You should obtain eventually a plan of the solution."
> "Have you seen it before? Or have you seen the same problem in a slightly different form? Do you know a
> related problem? Do you know a theorem that could be useful? Look at the unknown! And try to think of a
> familiar problem having the same or a similar unknown."
> "Here is a problem related to yours and solved before. Could you use it? Could you use its result? Could
> you use its method? Should you introduce some auxiliary element in order to make its use possible?"
> "Could you restate the problem? Could you restate it still differently? Go back to definitions."
> "If you cannot solve the proposed problem try to solve first some related problem. Could you imagine a
> more accessible related problem? A more general problem? A more special problem? An analogous problem?
> Could you solve a part of the problem? Keep only a part of the condition, drop the other part; how far is
> the unknown then determined, how can it vary? Could you derive something useful from the data? Could you
> think of other data appropriate to determine the unknown? Could you change the unknown or data, or both if
> necessary, so that the new unknown and the new data are nearer to each other?"
> "Did you use all the data? Did you use the whole condition? Have you taken into account all essential
> notions involved in the problem?"

生成子问题的显式规则：(a) 退到已解决的相关问题；(b) 复述/改述问题、回到定义；(c) 构造更易/更一般/更特殊
/类比问题；(d) 只解一部分（固定条件的一部分、丢弃另一部分，看未知量如何被约束/如何变化）；(e) 从数据端
反推能推出什么、需要什么别的数据才能确定未知量（从"目标"和"材料"两端同时逼近）。

其余两阶段的追问清单（任务要求逐条抄，一并列出）：
> **CARRYING OUT THE PLAN**（执行计划）："Carrying out your plan of the solution, check each step. Can you
> see clearly that the step is correct? Can you prove that it is correct?"
> **LOOKING BACK**（回顾）："Can you check the result? Can you check the argument? Can you derive the
> solution differently? Can you see it at a glance? Can you use the result, or the method, for some other
> problem?"

"回顾"阶段最后一问（"能否把这个结果或方法用到别的问题上"）本身也是一条生成规则：解出一题之后，用它去检验
能否覆盖其他问题，相当于反向探测"这道题的解法本身是否隐含着一个更一般问题的部分答案"。

## 7. Laudan，《Progress and Its Problems: Towards a Theory of Scientific Growth》(1977, UC Press)

**①出处**：Larry Laudan, *Progress and Its Problems: Towards a Theory of Scientific Growth*, University of
California Press, 1977（原始出版信息为学界公认书目事实，非本次核实）。实际打开的扫描件书名页显示为印度
Digital Library of India 项目收录的一个翻印本（"Ambika Publications, New Delhi"字样，书名页OCR质量差，
未见"University of California Press"或"1977"字样出现在这份扫描件的版权页上）：
https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.136278 （`_djvu.txt`；PDF本身是加密扫描图无文字层，改用
OCR文本）。以下引文的页码是这份扫描件内部连续的页码，取自正文页眉标注（如"ROLE OF EMPIRICAL PROBLEMS 15"），
不保证与 1977 年 UC Press 初版页码完全一致，但书名、作者、目录结构与正文论证均与公认的 Laudan 1977 一致。

**②核心对象与结论**：把科学定义为一种解题活动（problem-solving activity），理论的核心检验标准不是真/
确证，而是能否为重要问题提供满意解答：
> "Science is essentially a problem-solving activity." (p.11)
> Thesis 1: "The first and essential acid test for any theory is whether it provides acceptable answers to
> interesting questions: whether, in other words, it provides satisfactory solutions to important problems."
> (p.13)

**③好问题标准 / 前提不成立时怎么办**：本书不做单个问题层面的"预设检验"程序（这是它与 Collingwood/van
Fraassen 的关键差异——Laudan 关心的是"什么才算一个够格的问题/够格被纳入理论评价"，落点是问题的类型学，
不是单条问题的合法性诊断）。"够格算经验问题"本身就有筛选标准，且明确不要求为真——反常/虚构的"事实"
一样能构成经验问题：
> "A problem need not accurately describe a real state of affairs to be a problem: all that is required is
> that it be thought to be an actual state of affairs by some agent." （举例：早期学会相信海怪存在，海怪
> 的习性曾是正当的经验问题；19世纪初生物学家相信自转生殖，肉在阳光下变蛆也曾是正当的经验问题）(p.16)

并进一步要求"被感到有必要解释"（不只是被知道），以及"在特定时代因充分理由可以不再是问题"（p.17-18）——
即经验问题的资格本身是语境相对、历史可变的，不是世界直接给出的。概念问题的资格则明确二分，这本身就是
一条"什么算合法问题（的一种）"的生成规则：
> "Conceptual problems arise for a theory, T, in one of two ways: 1. When T exhibits certain internal
> inconsistencies, or when its basic categories of analysis are vague and unclear; these are internal
> conceptual problems. 2. When T is in conflict with another theory or doctrine, T', which proponents of T
> believe to be rationally well founded; these are external conceptual problems." (p.48-49)

**④生成下一问题的规则**：本书没有"上一题决定下一题问什么"意义上的链式生成规则（宏观科学史理论，不是
微观问答逻辑），但上面②中"概念问题因何而生"（内部不一致/概念含混 vs 与另一被信任理论冲突）本身就是一条
"什么条件下会生出一个新的（概念）问题"的规则，已如上引，不再重复。

**⑤待映射**：内部/外部概念问题的二分，可能对应 J++ 判断链中"这道题本身逻辑自洽吗"（内部）vs "这道题的
判断标准跟另一条已被信任的标准冲突吗"（外部）两类不同的"前提诊断"，待映射，不展开。

**⑥未核实**：external conceptual problems 的完整子类型列表（原书p.49起，只核实到分类学开头）；第3-8章
（问题解决效力的量化、与 Kuhn/Popper/Lakatos 的比较）完全未读。

## 8. Friedman，"Question-Directed Attitudes" (2013) 与 "The Epistemic and the Zetetic" (2020)

**①出处**：Jane Friedman, "Question-Directed Attitudes", *Philosophical Perspectives* 27, 2013, pp.145-174；
"The Epistemic and the Zetetic", *Philosophical Review* 129(4), 2020, pp.501-536。实际打开：作者主页PDF
https://jfriedmanphilo.github.io/QDA.pdf 、 https://jfriedmanphilo.github.io/E&Z.pdf
（均标注"请引用出版版本"）

**②核心对象与结论**：把好奇、疑惑、探究、调查、存疑这一组心理状态统一定义为"以问题为内容的态度"
(Interrogative Attitudes, IAs)：
> "they also all share another key feature: they all have questions (rather than propositions or something
> else) as contents." "they each, in some sense, presuppose ignorance on the relevant matter and are
> resolved or satisfied when a particular kind of new knowledge comes in." (QDA, p.1)
> "Questions are themselves incomplete or unresolved sorts of objects... the goal of inquiry (in very
> general terms) is to resolve this unresolved object – to answer a question." (QDA, p.23)

**③好问题标准 / 前提不成立时怎么办**：QDA 未涉及；EZ 提出探究的核心工具性规范 ZIP（zetetic instrumental
principle），处理的是"探究进行中该不该被无关证据打断"，不是"预设是否成立"：
> "ZIP If one wants to figure out Q?, then one ought to take the necessary means to figuring out Q?."
> "staying focused on a particular task often means avoiding certain kinds of distraction. And sometimes,
> following the evidence we already have and that's easily available to us down some 'inquiry-irrelevant'
> path can be just such a distraction." (EZ, p.2-3)

ZIP 与标准认知规范（有充分证据即可判断 EPa；能知道就可以去知道 KPa）之间的张力是全文核心论点：探究中途
"忽略无关但充分的证据"在 ZIP 下是恰当的，即便标准认知规范允许"去知道任何东西"。

**④生成下一问题的规则**：两篇均未给出链式生成规则。关于"何时该停止探究"，QDA 开篇给出的是唯一明确
表述——探究类态度"在某种特定的新知识到来时被消解或满足"：
> "they each, in some sense, presuppose ignorance on the relevant matter and are resolved or satisfied when
> a particular kind of new knowledge comes in." (QDA, p.1)

EZ 中呼应了同一说法（"move ourselves from ignorance to knowledge or from confusion to comprehension"，
p.2）。但这只是"探究在概念上何时算终结"（获得了那种知识），不是"该不该继续投入资源去获得它"这种操作性
停止判据。为确认后者是否被遗漏，另在 QDA/EZ/ZETEP 三篇全文中搜索"ignoran / ought not / already know /
end of inquiry / while believ"等词，除上述"消解于新知识到来"这一说法反复出现外，未发现任何更具体的
"何时该停止投入探究资源"的操作性判据。

**⑤待映射**：ZIP 的"聚焦-排除无关分心"规范，与 J++ 要求"每道题问的东西在面前的材料里吗"这条诊断在精神
上一致——都是在"有更多信息可用"和"该不该用在当前任务上"之间做取舍，待映射，不展开。

**⑥未核实**：EZ 中"何时该停止探究"的显式停止条件未找到（可能在 Friedman 其他论文，如 "Inquiry and
Belief" 2019 或 "Zetetic Epistemology" 2020 中有更直接论述，本次下载了后者 ZETEP.pdf 但搜索"stop"类
表述未命中）。

问题最通行的处理方式——否定一道题的预设，不算回答了这道题，而是算给出了一个不同种类的回应：
> "To deny a presupposition of a question is to give a _corrective answer_ to the question, but most
> theorists join Belnap and Steel in not counting corrective answers as direct answers."

即：direct answer（直接回答）与 corrective answer（纠正性回答）是两个不相交的范畴，后者不计入前者。这条
可以看作 Collingwood"does not arise"、Bromberger"contrary of a presupposition"、van Fraassen"问题被拒绝"
这三种各自独立发展出的机制，在当代问题逻辑标准表述里汇聚到的同一个分类学位置。
